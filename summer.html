About the Summer Edit

Every summer suit is cut from premium fine cotton lawn or lawn slub — light enough for Pakistani heat, dense enough to carry multi-head thread embroidery without puckering. Shirt pieces run 3 metres, dupattas 2.5 metres and trousers 2.5 metres.

All pieces arrive unstitched. Two-piece sets include shirt and trouser; three-piece sets add a matching voile or chiffon dupatta. Colours are photographed in daylight, though a slight variation between screens is normal.
