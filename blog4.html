5 Ways to Style Your Unstitched Suit on a Budget

20 October 2026 · 4 min read

Everyone loves a luxury look, but not everyone wants to spend a fortune on it. The secret to designer wear isn't always the price tag — it's how you style it.

If you've bought a beautiful unstitched suit but your budget for tailoring and accessories is limited, don't worry. With a few smart tricks, you can turn a simple lawn or khaddar suit into a high-end designer piece. Here are 5 budget-friendly ways to upgrade your look.

1. Upgrade with Laces and Borders

One of the easiest ways to add a luxury feel to a plain trouser or dupatta is by adding a lace (gota, organza, or embroidered border). A simple 2-inch lace at the bottom of your trouser or along the dupatta edges instantly makes the outfit look expensive. You can find beautiful laces in local markets for just Rs. 200–Rs. 300.

2. Change the Buttons

The buttons on a shirt can completely change its vibe. If your suit has plain plastic buttons, swap them for something more elegant like pearl buttons, metallic studs, or fabric-covered buttons. This small change costs very little but completely elevates the neckline.

3. Keep the Neckline Simple

If your suit already has heavy multi-head thread embroidery on the front panel, keep the neck design simple. A straight, V-neck, or a simple round neck with a slit is best. Over-designing the neck of an already embroidered suit makes it look cluttered and cheap. Let the fabric's embroidery be the star.

4. Focus on the Dupatta Styling

Instead of heavy embellishments on the dupatta, use styling to make it stand out. Pin it in an asymmetrical style, pleat it neatly on one shoulder, or use a contrasting piping edge. If your dupatta is plain chiffon, add a simple embroidered patch or a row of pearls along the border.

5. The Right Tailoring is Everything

Even the most expensive fabric looks cheap if the fitting is wrong. Since RANG DATA suits come unstitched with full lengths, you have the freedom to get it tailored perfectly. A well-fitted shirt, crisp ironing, and a neat hemline will make a budget suit look like it was made to measure.

Luxury is not about how much you spend — it's about how well you wear it.

By focusing on these details, you can enjoy the luxury look without breaking the bank. Browse our latest unstitched collection to find your next project, or read our guide on multi-head thread embroidery to understand the value of your fabric.
