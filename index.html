The RANG DATA way

Colour first, always

We start every design with the dye. A palette is chosen, fabric is sourced to hold it, and only then does the embroidery get drawn — multi-head industrial threadwork across the shirt front and sleeves.

Because every suit ships unstitched, you keep full shirt, dupatta and trouser lengths and take them to the tailor who already knows your fit.
