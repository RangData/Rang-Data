Orders are dispatched within 1 to 2 working days of confirmation. After dispatch, expect 3 to 5 working days for major cities such as Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Faisalabad, and 5 to 7 working days for smaller cities and rural areas. Public holidays and courier backlogs can add a day or two.
Send your order reference on WhatsApp and we will reply with the courier tracking number once the parcel is booked. Your reference is shown on screen right after you place the order, and it also appears in the WhatsApp summary you send us.
Everything we sell is unstitched. You receive full uncut lengths — shirt 3 metres, dupatta 2.5 metres and trouser 2.5 metres — so your own tailor can work to your measurements. Two-piece sets include shirt and trouser; three-piece sets add the matching dupatta.

You may request an exchange within 7 days of delivery if the fabric arrived with a manufacturing defect, or if the wrong article was sent. The fabric must be unused, uncut, unwashed and in its original packing. Notify us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery if the parcel arrives damaged, since courier claims have a short window.
Unstitched fabric cannot be returned for a change of mind. Once cloth has been cut, washed or sent to a tailor it cannot be resold, so exchanges are limited to defects and incorrect items. Please check the article code and colour on the product page before ordering.
