Lilen 3 Piece Printed Suits: Five New Designs

14 November 2026 · 3 min read

The RANG DATA Lilen collection is here: five digital-print suits, each a 3 piece unstitched set, at Rs. 2,300 per suit.

The fabric: Lilen

In this collection the shirt, dupatta and trouser are all cut from the same Lilen fabric, so the whole suit feels and falls the same way. Every suit comes unstitched with full lengths, so you can take it to the tailor who already knows your fit.

The five designs

  • LEN-101 — Mustard
  • LEN-102 — Black Floral
  • LEN-103 — Brown Floral
  • LEN-104 — Teal Tulip
  • LEN-105 — Dark Brown Lily

What you get

A 3 piece unstitched set with a digital print, priced at Rs. 2,300. We offer cash on delivery across Pakistan, and delivery is free when you order 3 items or more.

Styling and care

The print is the star, so keep the rest simple: light jewellery and plain accessories are enough. Wash gently in cold water, dry in the shade and iron from the reverse to keep the print looking fresh.
