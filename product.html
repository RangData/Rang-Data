Fabric & care

  • First wash cold and separately — deep dyes can bleed on the first wash.
  • Hand wash or use a gentle machine cycle. No bleach, no harsh detergent.
  • Dry in shade, turned inside out, to protect the embroidery and the colour.
  • Iron on medium heat from the reverse side. Avoid pressing directly onto threadwork.
  • Allow for a small amount of shrinkage on the first wash when giving lengths to your tailor.

Delivery & returns
