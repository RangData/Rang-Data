Noor-e-Rang Volume 01: Printed Winter Khaddar Suits

2 November 2026 · 3 min read

Noor-e-Rang means “light of colour”. Volume 01 is the first volume of our printed catalogue: five digital-print suits on winter khaddar, launched on 2 November 2026.

The fabric: Winter Khaddar

Khaddar has a visible texture and a matte finish, and it holds its shape well. It breathes, it does not feel heavy on the shoulders, and it suits daily wear, university and casual gatherings. In this volume the shirt, dupatta and trouser are all cut from the same khaddar, so the whole suit feels and falls the same way.

The five designs

  • CODE K 101 — Chocolate Brown, with golden flowers
  • CODE K 102 — Coral Orange, with a soft floral print
  • CODE K 103 — Black, with red and green leaves
  • CODE K 104 — Lilac Pink, with a floral print
  • CODE K 105 — Smoke Grey, with a floral print

What you get

Every suit is a 3-piece unstitched set with full shirt, dupatta and trouser lengths, so you can take it to the tailor who already knows your fit. Each design is Rs. 2,500, with cash on delivery across Pakistan and free delivery when you order 3 items or more.

Styling and care

The print is the star, so keep the rest simple: a plain shawl and minimal jewellery are enough. Wash cold, dry in the shade and iron from the reverse to keep the print looking fresh.
