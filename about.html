Our story

About RANG DATA

A Pakistani apparel house built on one idea: get the colour right, and everything else follows.

Who we are

Luxury unstitched, made properly

RANG DATA produces luxury unstitched 2-piece and 3-piece suits for women in Pakistan. Every article is designed in-house, embroidered on multi-head industrial machines and packed as full uncut lengths — shirt, dupatta and trouser — so the fit is yours to decide.

We keep our range deliberately small. Thirty live designs across two seasonal edits means we can check each one properly rather than push volume and hope.

How we work

Colour first, then cloth, then thread

Most brands pick a print and then find fabric for it. We do the opposite.

A season starts with a palette. Once a colour is settled, we source the base cloth that can actually hold that dye — premium fine cotton lawn and lawn slub for summer; heavy viscose, winter khaddar and warm dhank for the cold months. Only then is the embroidery drawn, so the threadwork is matched to the ground colour rather than dropped on top of it.

Shirt pieces run 3 metres, dupattas 2.5 metres and trousers 2.5 metres. Nothing is pre-cut and nothing is stitched, which is exactly how a good tailor prefers to receive it.

What we will not do

  • Sell a design we know is out of stock — sold-out articles are closed for ordering, not quietly backordered.
  • Photograph a suit in a way that flatters a colour it does not have.
  • Take payment online for stock we have not confirmed. Every order is verified on WhatsApp first.
What you get

Every order, the same standard

Full uncut lengths

Complete shirt, dupatta and trouser lengths as listed on every product page. No short-cutting the metreage.

Honest photography

Product shots are our own, taken in daylight. Screens vary, so a slight shift in tone is possible — the shape and the work will not surprise you.

A person on the other end

Order queries go to a human on WhatsApp between 10am and 8pm, not a ticket queue.
